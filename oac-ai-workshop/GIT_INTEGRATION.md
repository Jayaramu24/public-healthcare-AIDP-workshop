# OAC module integration

Integrated on 21 October 2026 under **Lab 8 - AI Powered Business Analytics** in the MPHA workshop.

The three-hour OAC runbook lives at `oac-ai-workshop/index.html`. Its Markdown labs, 76 unedited screenshots, metric checkpoints, semantic-model demo source and Claude MCP HTML/native workbook artifacts are published together. The main workshop retains its AIDP sequence and earlier OAC foundation steps as setup/reference material outside the 180-minute AI program.

Only synthetic MPHA data and reviewed teaching artifacts are included. Authentication state, wallet files, credentials, live Claude settings, the downloaded Oracle utility and preparation logs are excluded. Tenant names, object IDs and account/catalog references in the rehearsal evidence identify that specific reference environment; administrators must supply their own approved instance, dataset, accounts and permissions.

Run `python3 verify_package.py` here after any module change. Rebuild package-manifest.json and the OAC ZIP when files change. The root README and Lab 8 link to this module; the offline review bundle includes its ZIP.

Original rehearsal results remain dated 19-20 October 2026. Event readiness and product limitations remain in PREPARATION_STATUS.md and FACILITATOR.md; publication does not grant private OAC access or establish classroom capacity.
